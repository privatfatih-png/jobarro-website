Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Short version: Jobarro processes audio in real time and immediately discards it — nothing is recorded or stored. The stealth overlay is invisible to screen sharing at the Windows OS level. Your interview data never leaves your device.

1. Who we are

Jobarro is a Windows desktop application that provides real-time AI assistance during job interviews. We are the operator of jobarro.com and the Jobarro desktop application. For privacy questions, contact us at hello@jobarro.com.

2. What data we collect

When you make a purchase

Payments are handled by Stripe. When you buy a license, Stripe collects your email address and payment details. We receive your email address and a license key from Stripe so we can send you your purchase confirmation. We do not store your card number or any full payment details.

When you use the app

  • Audio: Jobarro captures your system audio via Windows WASAPI loopback. This audio is streamed in real time to Deepgram for transcription and is immediately discarded — it is never written to disk, stored on our servers, or retained in any form.
  • Transcribed text: The text of detected interview questions is sent to the Anthropic Claude API to generate answer suggestions. This text is processed in real time and not stored by Jobarro.
  • Session history: If you choose to review past sessions inside the app, a local summary (the question, answer type, and duration) is stored in a SQLite database on your device only. This data never leaves your machine and can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app.
  • License key: Your license key is stored locally on your device and sent to our license server to verify your subscription status. No other identifying information is transmitted.

When you visit our website

Our website (jobarro.com) loads fonts from Google Fonts. No tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels are set on this website. We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party behavioral tracking.

3. Audio processing — zero retention

Audio is streamed, never stored. Jobarro pipes your system audio directly to Deepgram's transcription API over an encrypted WebSocket connection. There is no intermediate recording step. Once transcription completes, the audio stream is discarded. Deepgram's own data retention policy applies to the transcription service; you can review it at deepgram.com/privacy.

We have no ability to replay, review, or access your audio or interview content — by design. Jobarro's servers never receive audio data.

4. The stealth overlay — how invisibility works

The Jobarro answer overlay is invisible to screen sharing software. This is not a trick or a hack — it uses an official Windows API:

  • The overlay window is created with the WS_EX_TOOLWINDOW extended window style, which excludes it from the taskbar and Alt+Tab.
  • The SetWindowDisplayAffinity function is called with the WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE flag (available on Windows 10 2004 and later). This tells the Windows compositor to exclude the window from any screen capture or recording.
  • Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and any other application that uses the Windows Desktop Duplication API or GDI capture will show a blank region where the overlay sits. Your interviewer sees only your face and whatever you share — the overlay is technically impossible for them to detect.

No screen content from your interviewer is ever captured, transmitted, or processed by Jobarro. The app only processes audio, not video.

5. Third-party services

Jobarro relies on the following third-party services during normal operation:

Service Purpose Data sent
Deepgram Real-time speech-to-text transcription Audio stream (discarded after transcription)
Anthropic AI-generated answer suggestions Transcribed question text + your role/industry profile
Stripe Payment processing Email address and payment details (handled directly by Stripe)
Railway License key verification server Your license key (to verify subscription status)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. Jobarro selects services that operate under industry-standard data protection practices and, where applicable, comply with GDPR.

6. Data storage and security

  • On your device: Session history summaries and app settings are stored locally in an SQLite database inside the Jobarro application data folder. Uninstalling the app removes this data.
  • On our servers: We store only your email address and license key status (active/expired). No audio, transcripts, or conversation data is stored server-side — ever.
  • In transit: All communication between Jobarro and external services (Deepgram, Anthropic, our license server) uses TLS 1.2+ encryption.

7. Data retention

  • Audio: Not retained. Zero retention.
  • Transcripts and AI answers: Not retained by Jobarro. Processed in real time and discarded.
  • Local session history: Retained on your device until you uninstall the app or clear it manually in settings.
  • Purchase records: Your email and license status are retained for as long as your subscription is active, and for up to 2 years afterward for support and accounting purposes.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR (EU/EEA), UK GDPR, CCPA (California), or similar laws. These include the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@jobarro.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

Jobarro is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are material, we will notify active license holders by email. Continued use of Jobarro after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns:

  • Email: hello@jobarro.com
  • Response time: We aim to respond within 2 business days
